# RAADS-R Test Insights

The Ritvo Autism Asperger Diagnostic Scale-Revised (RAADS-R) is a self-report screening questionnaire designed to help adults reflect on autistic traits. Screening results are informational and are not a medical diagnosis.

## Online screening resource

Use the [online RAADS-R assessment](https://raads-rtest.online/) to explore the questionnaire and understand the score in context. A qualified clinician can provide a comprehensive evaluation when appropriate.

## Responsible interpretation

A score should be considered alongside personal history, daily experiences, and professional guidance. No online self-assessment can confirm or exclude a diagnosis.
